Values & Faith

Faith & Values

The Maguindanao community in Poblacion, Lapuyan reflects a rich understanding of faith, cooperation, and shared responsibility. Islamic faith is part of the community life of many families, alongside values such as respect, hospitality, honesty, and care for elders and neighbors.

Community practices are shaped by mutual support, family bonds, and peaceful relationships. Education, responsibility, and respect for elders strengthen the continuity of tradition and cultural identity.

  • Faith
  • Respect
  • Family
  • Cooperation
  • Hospitality
  • Responsibility
  • Peace
  • Respect for Elders

Traditional Music

Traditional Music

KULINTANG

Set of tuned gongs used in ensemble music and community performance, often central to cultural gatherings.

AGUNG

Large hanging gong that adds resonance and depth to traditional music performances.

DABAKAN

Drum instrument that provides rhythm, coordination, and pulse in ensembles.

Traditional Food

Traditional Food

Dudol

Ingredients: Local ingredients, rice, coconut, or family-prepared components.

Preparation: Examples vary among households and communities.

Cultural importance: Food traditions often highlight gathering and hospitality.

Tapay

Ingredients: Fermented rice or millet-based ingredients, depending on local practice.

Preparation: Traditional preparation may vary by household and community source.

Cultural importance: Shared during family and community moments.

Kilaw

Ingredients: Fresh ingredients prepared with local seasoning and ingredients.

Preparation: Demonstrates how local food traditions are shaped by access and preference.

Cultural importance: Part of everyday meals and community gatherings.
